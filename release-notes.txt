Version 1.6.10

• Reverts Retrieve to the same neutral macOS toolbar treatment as the other controls.
• Rebuilds toolbar groups as explicit rounded pill controls so their borders and backgrounds remain visible in the title bar.
• Adds vertical divider lines inside Paste/Open, CSV/Transcript, and Threads/Messages pills, matching the grouped-control treatment used by Apple Mail.
• Gives Settings a compact circular/rounded bubble and Retrieve a wider neutral pill.
• Adds extra outer padding to every custom toolbar item so both the live toolbar and the Customize Toolbar palette have more Apple Mail-like breathing room.
• Keeps the native macOS toolbar customizer, drag-and-drop rearrangement, Space, Flexible Space, display modes, and automatic layout persistence.
