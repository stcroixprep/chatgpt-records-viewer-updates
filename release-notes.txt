Version 1.6.6

• Makes Retrieve its own standalone double-wide toolbar button instead of grouping it with Paste and Open.
• Adds a Customize Toolbar window for showing, hiding, and reordering toolbar groups.
• Adds Apple Mail-style toolbar display choices: Icon and Text, Icon Only, and Text Only.
• Adds Customize Toolbar… to the toolbar context menu and Settings > Appearance.
• Preserves the default grouped pills for Paste/Open, CSV/Transcript, and Threads/Messages while allowing the overall toolbar layout to be rearranged.
