Version 1.6.22

• Adds a Both Platforms option in Retrieve to collect ChatGPT and Claude staff conversation records concurrently into one searchable view.
• Preserves source-specific transcript labels, attachments, and CSV/HTML exports when results from both providers are combined.
• Uses green checked boxes throughout the retrieval and search controls, with matching green connection-success indicators.
• Adds an optional Settings checkbox to offer the Retrieve window automatically after launch/unlock and after clearing a search or closing a conversation preview.
• The automatic prompt is disabled by default and does not immediately reopen when canceled.
• Keeps ChatView's existing macOS bundle identifier, Keychain security, in-place Sparkle update feed, and signed release process.

The combined view is on-demand retrieval, not a continuous records archive or legal-hold system.
