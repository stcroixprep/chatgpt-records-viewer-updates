Version 1.6.17

• Removes "Add Flexible Space" from the toolbar right-click menu, leaving the display-style choices and Customize Toolbar… option.
• Hides the main-window staff-email, search, and date filter panel until a Retrieve search finishes successfully.
• Keeps the filters visible after a successful retrieval, even if the search returns no matching records.
• Makes Cancel available during an active retrieval. Cancel now interrupts the underlying Compliance Platform network task, dismisses the retrieval window, and prevents late responses from overwriting existing results.
• Prevents canceled retrievals from changing the currently applied search filters.
• Renames the Retrieve & Filter button to Retrieve.
• Preserves the native unified toolbar, Touch ID, Keychain credentials, and existing signed update channel.
