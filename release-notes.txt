Version 1.6.7

• Makes the toolbar customizer feel more like Apple Mail and other macOS apps.
• Adds a fixed Space item and a Flexible Space item for toolbar layout.
• Allows multiple Space and Flexible Space items to be inserted, reordered, and removed.
• Shows a live Current Toolbar preview so spacing choices are easier to understand.
• Fixed Space creates a consistent empty gap; Flexible Space expands to push later controls toward the far side.
• Keeps Icon and Text, Icon Only, Text Only, show/hide controls, reordering, and Restore Default Set.
